## 📜 Documented Nepalese Data Sources & Citations

### 1. Geospatial & Tourism (`instruction/domain/geospatial_tourism/` & `trekking/`)
- **[BlazeWild/TrekNepal-3B](https://github.com/BlazeWild/TrekNepal-3B__Finetuned-Llama3.2-3B)** — Synthetic Q&A dataset generation on Nepalese trekking routes.
- **`nepal_master_inventory.csv`** — 524 geographic landmarks, administrative divisions, and natural attractions across 7 provinces of Nepal.
- **[bedbyaspokhrel/nepal-geo-data](https://github.com/bedbyaspokhrel/nepal-geo-data)** & **[GunSliger00007/nepali-address-py](https://github.com/GunSliger00007/nepali-address-py)** — Nepal's 77 districts, 7 provinces, and local-level administrative boundary metadata.

### 2. Agriculture & Employment (`instruction/domain/agriculture/`)
- **[dhakalasim/nepal-crop-predictor](https://github.com/dhakalasim/nepal-crop-predictor)** — Climate and elevation-based crop prediction models for Nepalese districts.
- **[Apeksha04-ai/SundarBagaichaa](https://github.com/Apeksha04-ai/Apeksha_Neupane_SundarBagaichaa)** — Farmer machinery, agricultural inputs, and seasonal weather advice.
- **[renu-j/Nepali-Job-Portal-Scraper-](https://github.com/renu-j/Nepali-Job-Portal-Scraper-)** — Nepalese job portal listings dataset for career guidance.
- **[suman-goit/ML-based-diet-planner-using-nepali-dataset](https://github.com/suman-goit/ML-based-diet-planner-using-nepali-dataset)** — Nepalese food recipes, ingredients, and nutrition planner dataset.

### 3. Script Conversion & Transliteration (`instruction/script_conversion/`)
- **[Shahil050/romanized_nepali_dataset](https://github.com/Shahil050/romanized_nepali_dataset)** — Parallel Romanized Nepali to Devanagari text dataset.
- **[SushilShrestha/NepaliTransliteralDataset](https://github.com/SushilShrestha/NepaliTransliteralDataset)** — Open-source benchmark dataset for automatic transliteration.

### 4. Code-Switched Speech ASR (`instruction/asr/`)
- **[ishworrsubedii/nepali_speech-to-text_datasets](https://github.com/ishworrsubedii/nepali_speech-to-text_datasets)** — Collection of Nepali audio recordings and transcriptions.
- **[Aadarshttech/nepali-english-codeswitched-whisper](https://github.com/Aadarshttech/nepali-english-codeswitched-whisper)** — Fine-tuning dataset schema for adapting OpenAI Whisper to Nepali-English code-switched speech recognition.

### 5. Legal & Public Governance (`instruction/domain/legal/`)
- **[Joshiexzz/NepalLegalDataset](https://github.com/Joshiexzz/NepalLegalDataset)** — Scraped court case decisions, legal provisions, and CIAA annual corruption reports.
