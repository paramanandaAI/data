## 🧪 Fair Prompting & Evaluation Protocol for NLU

- **Probing Tasks**: Regular vs. Irregular Sandhi Morphotactics, 91 & 112 POS Tagging Accuracy, NER Extraction, Legal RAG Answer Accuracy.
- **Baselines**: Rule Engine (Bal 2007), CFG Parser (Recall: 81.71%, Precision: 72.38%), FST Transducer (Prasain 2011).
