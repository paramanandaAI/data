# Nepali Clinical & Healthcare NLP: Research & Linguistic Guide

## 🇳🇵 Nepali Healthcare Linguistics & Patient Colloquialisms
1. **Colloquial Symptom Descriptions vs. Medical Terminology:**
   - Patient query: *मुटु ढुकढुक भयो* (Heart palpitations), *टाउको फनफनी घुम्यो* (Vertigo/Dizziness), *पेट बटारियो* (Abdominal cramping).
   - Clinical Devanagari translation: *उच्च रक्तचाप* (Hypertension), *मधुमेह* (Diabetes), *श्वासप्रश्वास समस्या* (Respiratory illness).
2. **Honorific Bedside Manner:** Doctor responses must employ High Honorific (*हजुर / तपाईँ*) with imperative suggestions ending in *...गर्नुहोस् / ...नखानुहोस्*.
