# Nepali Agriculture & Agro-Advisory NLP: Research & Linguistic Guide

## 🇳🇵 Agro-Ecological Zones & Local Dialectal Phrasing
1. **Three Ecological Zones:** Advice differs fundamentally across **Terai (तराई)**, **Hilly (पहाडी)**, and **Himalayan (हिमाली)** regions.
2. **Crop Cycles & Calendar (बिक्रम संवत्):** References to planting seasons must adhere to Nepali BS months:
   - *असार/साउन* (Monsoon paddy transplantation), *मंसिर/पुष* (Winter wheat/mustard), *फागुन/चैत* (Spring maize).
3. **Pest & Disease Folk Names:**
   - *गवारो कीरा* (Stem borer), *डढुवा रोग* (Late blight in potatoes), *खुम्रे कीरा* (White grub).

## 🤖 LLM Recommendation Guidelines
- Structured generation should output: **बाली (Crop)**, **मौसम (Season)**, **माटोको प्रकार (Soil Type)**, **मलखाद (Fertilizer dosage)**, and **रोग नियन्त्रण (Pest Management)**.
