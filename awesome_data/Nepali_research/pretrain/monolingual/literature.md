## 📚 Academic Literature & Citations

1. **NepBERTa: Nepali Pretrained Language Model**
   - **Authors:** Shushanta Timilsina, Rojan Gautam, & Prabin Bhattarai (2022)
   - **Venue:** *Proceedings of AfricaNLP @ ACL Anthology*
   - **Description:** Pretraining RoBERTa on 1.5B tokens of scraped news and web text; established baseline for masked language modeling in Nepali.

2. **IndicBERT & IndicCorp: Multilingual Pretrained Models for Indic Languages**
   - **Authors:** Divyanshu Kakwani, Anoop Kunchukuttan, Satish Golla, et al. (2020)
   - **Venue:** *Findings of EMNLP 2020*
   - **Description:** Large-scale 12-language pretraining corpora including Nepali Devanagari web corpora and ALBERT/BERT model variants.

3. **CC-100: Monolingual Datasets from Web Crawl**
   - **Authors:** Guillaume Wenzek, Marie-Anne Lachaux, Alexis Conneau, et al. (2020)
   - **Venue:** *ACL 2020*
   - **Description:** CommonCrawl automated extraction pipeline for low-resource languages (`cc100-ne`).

4. **Large-Scale Nepali Text Corpus**
   - **Authors:** Rabindra Lamsal (2020)
   - **Venue:** *IEEE DataPort*, DOI: [10.21227/h5e8-wz41](https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/large-scale-nepali-text-corpus)
   - **Description:** 90 million running words from news portals with pre-trained word embeddings.

5. **Nepali National Corpus (NNC / NELRALEC)**
   - **Authors:** Yogendra P. Yadava, Govinda Raj Bhattarai, Balaram Prasain, et al. (2008)
   - **Venue:** *NELRALEC Project Report & ELRA Catalog*
   - **Description:** First national standard corpus containing 14M words across 15 genres, including core written sample (802k words) and general text (1.4M words).
