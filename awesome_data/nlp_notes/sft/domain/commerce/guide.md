# Nepali Commerce, Banking & Retail NLP: Research & Linguistic Guide

## 🇳🇵 Financial & Stock Market Linguistics
1. **NEPSE Specific Jargon:**
   - *परिसूचक* (Index), *कारोबार रकम* (Turnover), *बजार पुँजीकरण* (Market Capitalization), *लाभांश / बोनस सेयर* (Dividend/Bonus Share), *हकप्रद सेयर* (Right Share), *धितोपत्र बोर्ड* (SEBON).
2. **Nepali Currency & Number Formatting:**
   - South Asian numbering system: **हजार (1,000)**, **लाख (1,00,000)**, **करोड (1,00,00,000)**, **अरब (1,00,00,00,000)**.
   - Comma placement: `१२,३४,५६७` (12 Lakh 34 Thousand 567).
