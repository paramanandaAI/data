## 📜 Documented Nepalese Monolingual Text Corpora & Data Sources

### 1. General Web & News Corpora
- **[superryeti/Daraz-online-shopping-data-corpus](https://github.com/superryeti/Daraz-online-shopping-data-corpus)** — Scraped product reviews, descriptions, and metadata corpus from `daraz.com.np`.
- **[shekharkoirala/nepali_wikipedia_curpos](https://github.com/shekharkoirala/nepali_wikipedia_curpos)** — Raw and cleaned text dumps of Nepali Wikipedia articles.
- **[anurag3345/NepaliCleanedDataset](https://github.com/anurag3345/NepaliCleanedDataset)** — Large cleaned text corpus for training monolingual Nepali transformer models.
- **[ashokbasnet/nepali-news-dataset](https://github.com/ashokbasnet/nepali-news-dataset)** — Nepali news corpus covering 10 categories from 5 popular portals (OnlineKhabar, Setopati, Ratopati, Kantipur, Nagarik).
- **[Utshav-paudel/Nepali-Media-Content](https://github.com/Utshav-paudel/Nepali-Media-Content)** — Multi-source scraped media content from Nepalese online portals.

### 2. Pre-processing & Data Ingestion Pipelines
- **[NansKong/Nepali-data-pipeline](https://github.com/NansKong/Nepali-data-pipeline)** — Data processing pipeline for extracting and cleaning raw Nepali text corpora.
- **[shiva500/Meta-llama3-data-prep-english-to-Nepali-converter-](https://github.com/shiva500/Meta-llama3-data-prep-english-to-Nepali-converter-)** — Data preprocessing scripts for adapting LLaMA 3 tokenizers and tokenizing Nepali text corpora.
- **[acharyabi/Text-Data-Cleaning](https://github.com/acharyabi/Text-Data-Cleaning)** — Pre-processing text data cleaning scripts tested on Devanagari text.
- **[openknowledgenp/opendatahandbook-nepali](https://github.com/openknowledgenp/opendatahandbook-nepali)** — Nepali translation of Open Data principles and guidelines.
